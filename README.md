# HYD26_Actrip

**Actrip** is a integrated mobility app proposed for [Hack Your District 2026 (HYD 2026)](https://www.tu.berlin/arte/forschung/projekte/hackyourdistrict/hackyourdistrict-2026).

Our goal is to explore how different mobility options can be integrated into a more accessible, seamless, and sustainable travel experience.

The project is currently in the conceptualization phase. Features, technical implementation, and the overall direction are still being explored.

## About HYD 2026

Learn more about the initiative on the [official Hack Your District 2026 website](https://www.tu.berlin/arte/forschung/projekte/hackyourdistrict/hackyourdistrict-2026).
