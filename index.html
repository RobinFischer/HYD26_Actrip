Track. Ride. Discover.

acTrip is a trip planner for Taichung that puts bus, MRT, YouBike and walking on one map. Every bike route gets a bikability score, so you can see how comfortable the ride will be and pick a more pleasant route when the shortest one isn't.

Open the map How to use it

What's in the app

One map of Taichung with everything you need to get from A to B without a car.

Trip planner

Compare four ways to travel: Fastest (bus, metro and bike combined), Transit, Bike with YouBike, and Walk. Add stops on the way and drag the start and end pins.

Bikability score

Roads are rated from 1★ to 5★ for cycling based on their width and the green space beside them. Bike routes show their average score and flag red sections.

Transit and YouBike layers

Bus stops and routes, TRA, MRT and High Speed Rail stations, and YouBike stations with live bike and dock counts where available.

How to plan and navigate a route

Everything happens in the panel on the left of the map (at the bottom on phones).

  1. Set your start and destination

    Under Plan a trip, type a place, stop or station into the From (A) and To (B) fields and pick a suggestion. You can also press Pick on map and click the map, or use My location to start from where you are.

  2. Adjust the trip

    • Drag the A and B pins on the map to fine-tune the points.
    • + Add stop adds a place to visit on the way.
    • Swap reverses the trip, Clear starts over.

  3. Choose how to travel

    The four tabs show the best time for each mode:

    • Fastest: bus, metro and YouBike combined, quickest first.
    • Transit: bus and metro with walking.
    • Bike: ride a YouBike between the stations nearest to A and B.
    • Walk: on foot the whole way.

  4. Open an option and follow it on the map

    Click an option (Option 1, Option 2, …) to expand its step-by-step legs: where to walk, which bus or MRT to board and where to get off, and which YouBike station to rent from and return to. The selected route is drawn on the map, and walking and cycling legs follow the actual streets.

    Times are estimates: buses have no timetable in the app, and live YouBike counts can change before you arrive, so check before you go.

  5. Save it as a favourite

    Under Favourite routes, type a name and press Save. The start, destination, stops and travel mode are kept in your browser together with the route's bike rating. Choose a favourite from the list to reload the whole trip; its saved rating shows straight away and is updated when the route is rated again.

How to read the bikability score

The score tells you how comfortable a road is for cycling, from 1★ (narrow, avoid if you can) to 5★ (wide or next to green space).

Road scores

Turn on Bikability score under Layers and zoom in to street level. Roads are coloured by score; tap one to see its score and why.

  • 1★under 4 m wide
  • 2★4–6 m
  • 3★6–10 m
  • 4★10–15 m
  • 5★15 m or wider
  • No datacounted as 2.5★

Width is measured, or estimated from lanes, road name and bus routes. Roads within 30 m of a park, or with 25% or more greenery beside them in satellite imagery, score 4–5★.

Bike route ratings

In the Bike tab, each option shows a rating: the average road score along the ride, weighted by distance, plus the share of the ride on 4–5★ roads.

  • Red sections are 1★ stretches, marked with a warning sign on the map. Ride them with care.
  • Pleasant route: if the shortest route has red sections or averages below 3.5★, acTrip looks for an alternative close by with no red sections and 3.5★ or better, and shows how much longer the detour is.
  • A warning appears when a route rates below 2★ overall.
Your feedback changes the scores. Rate a whole ride with Good / Bad under its rating, or tap any section of a route on the map to rate just that road. Votes are saved in your browser and count the next time routes are rated. To download or clear them, press Show debug info at the bottom of the panel.
