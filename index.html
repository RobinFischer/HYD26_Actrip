Built for active urban movement

Actrip turns city navigation into a healthier, more comfortable experience by balancing convenience, safety, and environmental impact.

🚲

Bike-first routing

Prioritize cyclist-friendly paths, low-traffic segments, and connected mobility networks so biking feels natural, efficient, and enjoyable.

🛣️

Road condition awareness

Surface quality, potholes, construction areas, and steep or uneven segments are surfaced before the rider reaches them, helping people choose the best route.

⚠️

Obstacle-aware navigation

Temporary barriers, narrow passages, and blocked lanes are flagged in real time so navigation adjusts instantly during the trip.
